Updates you can undo.

New versions reach everyone without a new download. AudioTwizz asks first and checks every file. If a new version misbehaves, one button takes you back to any of the last three versions.

Go back to an older version

Open Settings, then About, press Go back to an older version and pick one of the last three. Your settings are backed up first, and the newer version isn’t offered again until you check for updates yourself.

Checks on its own, asks once

Checked before it’s installed

Set up in five steps.

Discord needs an "application" to show your music under. It's free and takes about two minutes.

  1. Run the installer

    It installs into your user folder, adds a Desktop and Start menu shortcut, and installs Python if you don't have it. No admin rights needed.

  2. Create a Discord application

    Open the Discord Developer Portal, sign in, and choose New Application. Its name is what shows after "Listening to" on your profile.

  3. Copy the Application ID

    On the General Information page, copy the Application ID. It's a long number made only of digits.

  4. Paste it into AudioTwizz

    Paste it in the installer when it asks, or later in Settings, then press Save.

  5. Press play

    Start any music with the Discord desktop app open. Your status shows up within a couple of seconds.

Before you install: cover art is uploaded by default. To show a cover from your own files, AudioTwizz uploads a small 512x512 copy to an anonymous image host, and anyone with the link can see it. To opt out, open Settings, then Display and switch off Upload covers from my files so Discord can show them. See everything that leaves your PC.

Antivirus warnings: Because AudioTwizz isn't code signed yet, a few antivirus engines may show a false detection on the installer. Open the VirusTotal report to see exactly which engines and what they say, and compare your download's SHA-256 with the one above to confirm it's the real file.

What leaves your PC.

Your music files stay on your computer. To show a status, AudioTwizz does send a few things over the internet. Here is exactly what, and how to switch it off.

Your status goes to Discord

The song title, artist, album and progress, so Discord can show them on your profile.

Cover art is uploaded to an image host

Discord can only show a picture it can download itself. So a cover read from your file is shrunk to 512x512 and uploaded to an anonymous image host (litterbox, catbox, uguu, tmpfiles or 0x0.st), and the link is passed to Discord. Anyone with that link can see the image. This is on by default. Turn it off in Settings with Upload covers from my files so Discord can show them.

Missing details are looked up online

The song title and artist are sent to iTunes and Deezer to fill in missing covers, albums and lengths. Switch it off with Fill in missing covers, albums and lengths. Links for the "Open this song" button come from song.link.

Update checks go to our server

The app asks the update server whether a newer version exists, and which older versions you can go back to. Your settings, including your Application ID, stay in a folder on your PC.

Status not showing?

Four quick checks fix most problems. Answer each one and the fix appears under the step that needs it.

  1. Is AudioTwizz running?Look for it in the tray, next to the clock.

    Open it first. Start AudioTwizz from the Desktop or Start menu shortcut. It closes to the tray, so check there too. Once it is open, make sure it says presence is on.

  2. Is “Display current activity” on in Discord?Discord settings, Activity Privacy.

    Turn it on. In the Discord desktop app, open User Settings, then Activity Privacy, and switch on the option that shows your current activity. Then pause and play your song again.

  3. Does your song show in the Windows volume flyout?Press a volume key and look for the song name above the slider.

    Your player is not telling Windows. AudioTwizz reads the same media controls that flyout uses. Turn on the media controls option in your player (some players need a plugin for it), or try a player that reports to Windows, like VLC or MusicBee.

  4. Is privacy mode off?The hide hotkey, or rules for chosen apps, fullscreen and quiet hours.

    Your status is being hidden on purpose. Press Ctrl+Alt+H to toggle the hide hotkey, then check Settings for rules that hide your status while chosen apps run, in fullscreen, or during quiet hours.

Common questions

Nothing shows on my profile

Check that the Discord desktop app is running, that AudioTwizz shows presence as on, and that Discord's Activity Privacy setting allows sharing your activity. You also can't see your own link buttons or some details on your own profile, so ask a friend to look.

It says "Add your Application ID"

No Application ID is saved yet. Paste it in Settings and press Save first.

It says "Waiting for Discord to open"

AudioTwizz can't find the Discord desktop app. Open Discord, and AudioTwizz connects on its own a few seconds later.

It says "Presence is off"

AudioTwizz isn't sending your status right now. Switch presence back on in the app.

It says it couldn't upload the cover

None of the image hosts answered. Your status still works without a cover, and it tries again on the next song. You can also turn off cover upload in Settings.

It says "Invalid Application ID"

The ID is wrong. Copy it again from General Information in the Developer Portal. It must be digits only, with no spaces.

Do I need Python?

The installer looks for a compatible Python and installs one for you if it can't find it. This happens once.

Does it work with Spotify and my browser?

Yes. Anything that reports to the Windows media controls works, and streaming songs get a button that opens the song.

Can I hide my status?

Yes. Press Ctrl+Alt+H, or set it to hide automatically while certain apps run, in fullscreen, or at set quiet hours.

How do updates work?

AudioTwizz checks for a new version on start and every six hours, asks before installing, then closes and reopens by itself. You never need to download the installer again.

Can I go back to an older version?

Yes. Open Settings, then About, press Go back to an older version, and pick one of the last three versions. Your settings are backed up first, and AudioTwizz won't offer the newer version again until you check for updates yourself.

How do I remove it?

Run AudioTwizzUninstall.exe from the download section above.

Need help or found a bug?

Tried the checklist above? Message me on Discord. In AudioTwizz open Settings, then About, press Copy diagnostic information and paste it in your message.
